# QUEUE

FIFO (First In First Out)

Can be implemented with a linked-list.

Elements are pushed at the tail, popped from the head.

Insertion and deletion – O(1)

Space complexity – O(n)

## Corresponds to (STL) std::queue:

Can be implemented as a list or deque.

Iterators are not provided.

q.push(obj), q.pop(), q.emplace(obj), q.front(), q.back(), q.empty(), q.size(), …
