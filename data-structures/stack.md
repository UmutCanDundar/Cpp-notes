# STACK

LIFO (Last In First Out)
Can be implemented with a vector or linked-list.
Operations occur on the head for linked-lists and the back for vectors.
Insertion and deletion – O(1)
Space complexity – O(n)

## Corresponds to (STL) std::stack:

Can be implemented as a vector, list or deque.
Iterators are not provided.
s.push(obj), s.pop(), s.emplace(obj), s.top(), s.empty(), s.size(), …
